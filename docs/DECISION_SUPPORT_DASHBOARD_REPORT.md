# Dam-Break Decision-Support Dashboard Report

## Purpose
The **Dam-Break Decision-Support Dashboard** translates raw multi-gigabyte 2D shallow-water hydrodynamic simulation rasters into actionable, physically sound, and easily interpretable decision-support indicators for SIH evaluation.

Rather than expecting downstream responders, engineers, or evaluation panels to inspect raw NetCDF/GeoTIFF files, the dashboard extracts core hydrodynamic summaries:
1. **Inundation Extent & Coverage**: Where and how much domain area was submerged.
2. **Hydraulic Peaks**: Maximum modeled water depth ($h$) and depth-averaged flow velocity ($v$).
3. **Arrival Timing**: Progression of first water arrival downstream ($t_{\text{arrival}}$), separating initial reservoir wet cells from newly inundated downstream floodplains.
4. **Hydraulic Severity Index**: Spatial product of depth and velocity ($H = h \times v$) as a demonstration proxy for hydrodynamic intensity.
5. **Downstream Longitudinal Progression**: Reach-based downstream distance bands assessing hydraulic attenuation.
6. **Modeled Critical Points**: Specific geographic coordinate locations with extreme modeled hydraulic values.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scientific Disclaimer**: This dashboard is an engineering and scientific demonstration tool developed for Smart India Hackathon (SIH). It is **not** an operational early warning system, evacuation dispatch tool, or regulatory flood hazard map. All outputs are derived from uncalibrated demonstration scenarios on open-source digital elevation data (SRTM ~30 m).

---

## Data Sources
The decision-support system operates deterministically on outputs generated by regional hydrodynamic simulations:
* **Digital Elevation Model**: SRTM 30 m DEM clipped and reprojected to project UTM CRS (e.g., EPSG:32643).
* **Dam Geometry & Reservoir**: Synthetic or polygon-delineated reservoir boundaries with dam crest/base elevations.
* **Hydrodynamic Rasters**:
  - `depth_max.tif`: Maximum modeled water depth per cell over all simulation timesteps ($m$).
  - `velocity_max.tif`: Maximum modeled depth-averaged flow velocity per cell ($m/s$).
  - `arrival_time.tif`: Earliest simulation time when water depth in a cell exceeded the wet threshold ($h \ge 0.05\text{ m}$) ($s$).
  - `hydraulic_severity.tif`: Derived raster computing cell-wise $H = h_{\max} \times v_{\max}$ ($m^2/s$).
* **Run Metadata**: Simulation execution parameters (`run.json`), including time duration, timestep interval, Manning roughness ($n$), breach geometry, and mesh resolution.

---

## Primary Solver
* **Primary Regional Analysis**: **ANUGA 2D Shallow-Water Simulation**
  - Solves the 2D depth-averaged non-linear shallow-water equations (SWE) using finite-volume shock-capturing schemes over an unstructured triangular mesh.
  - Responsible for all regional flood routing, inundation extent, downstream travel times, and decision-support metrics across the multi-kilometer study area.
* **Supplementary Near-Field View**: **Custom Terrain-SPH Demonstration**
  - Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) provides particle-based near-field visualization of initial crest breach collapse and 2D depth-integrated fluid discharge dynamics in the immediate dam vicinity.
  - SPH results serve as an educational cross-validation and near-field demonstration, and do **not** govern regional floodplain decision metrics.

## Arrival-Time Analysis
Flood arrival times are determined from cell-wise activation ($h \ge 0.05\text{ m}$):
* **Reservoir Exclusion**: Cells with water present at $t=0$ (the initial reservoir pool) are excluded from downstream arrival statistics to prevent $0\text{ s}$ arrival time bias.
* **Downstream Flood Wave Progression**:
  - The leading edge of the breach wave travels the first $1.09\text{ km}$ downstream in approximately $60\text{ s}$ ($\sim 18.2\text{ m/s}$ wave celerity).
  - Broad valley inundation spreads progressively over $24\text{ min}$ ($1440\text{ s}$) across secondary swales and tributary junctions.

## Hydraulic Severity Method
Hydraulic severity is evaluated on a cell-by-cell basis using the depth-velocity product:
$$H = h_{\max} \times v_{\max} \quad [\text{m}^2/\text{s}]$$

## UI Architecture
The Decision-Support Dashboard is embedded into `DamOnboardingPanel.tsx` as Step 5 (`📊 5. Decision Support`):
1. **Top Banner**: Scenario definition, solver distinction, and quick-export controls (JSON / CSV).
2. **KPI Card Grid**: 5 high-contrast KPI metric cards with units, values, and interpretive explanations.
3. **Primary Hazard Map**:
   - Dynamic raster tile visualization powered by MapLibre GL.
   - Mode Selector buttons: `DEPTH`, `VELOCITY`, `ARRIVAL TIME`, `HYDRAULIC SEVERITY`.
   - Interactive Critical Point Markers showing popups with exact coordinates, metrics, and non-observational disclaimers.
   - Continuous color ramps and legend keys with unit markers.
4. **Hydraulic Distributions**: Side-by-side SVG bar charts displaying water depth and velocity area distributions.
5. **Downstream Distance Reach Analysis**: Structured table depicting hydraulic attenuation along the river corridor.
6. **Interpretive Context ("What This Means")**: Neutral explanations of depth, velocity, arrival time, and severity index.
7. **SPH vs ANUGA Comparison Shortcut**: One-click navigation to the dedicated SPH vs ANUGA scientific comparison panel.
8. **Limitations & Governance Notice**: Prominent display of modeling assumptions, digital elevation limits, and non-operational notices.

## Limitations
* **DEM Resolution**: Global SRTM 1-arcsecond (~30 m grid) does not resolve narrow sub-grid drainage ditches, embankments, culverts, or bridge piers.
* **Breach Mechanics**: Instantaneous partial or trapezoidal breach geometries represent hypothetical conservative dam-break scenarios rather than geotechnical progressive piping models.
* **Roughness Parameterization**: Uniform Manning roughness coefficient ($n = 0.035$) is assumed without empirical flood gauge calibration.
* **Hydrological Inflow**: Dry downstream riverbed conditions assumed at $t=0$; no baseflow or rainfall-runoff integration.
* **Non-Operational Status**: Intended strictly for demonstration, engineering scenario screening, and educational visualization.

---

## Appropriate Interpretation
* Results provide comparative insight into spatial flood propagation, peak hydrodynamic loading zones, and wave arrival velocity across a hypothetical scenario.
* Values must not be interpreted as certified predictions of specific real-world disaster events without high-resolution LiDAR, geotechnical breach calibration, and hydrological validation.
